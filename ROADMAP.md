# Reachwick roadmap

## Product goal

Build an open-source workspace for both prospect discovery and lead management. Guided discovery should produce source-attributed prospects owners can review and add to the existing lead workflow. People can run Reachwick locally or on any server that supports Docker Compose and persistent storage.

## 0.1 — Lead and outreach management

Manual entry and CSV import/export for leads, persistent lead management, draft campaigns, ordered sequences, schedules, suppressions, settings, owner authentication, and portable deployment.

## Next — Lead discovery for the owner workflow

Build a guided prospect discovery and research workflow that supports lead generation as well as management. Track source and review generated results before they enter the lead list; keep external research providers optional.

## Later

Live delivery with suppression enforcement, mailbox sync, integrations, multi-user roles, and optional billing. These require separate designs and are not promised by current controls.
